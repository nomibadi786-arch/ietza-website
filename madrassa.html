Madrassa Rahmaniyyah

Deeni Ta'leem for Zambian students, part of the Islamic Educational Trust of Zambia (IETZA), Great East Road, Lusaka.

Introduction

Our Origins and Purpose

Madrassa Rahmaniyyah was founded in 1976 to provide Deeni Ta'leem (Islamic education) to needy and underprivileged indigenous Zambians. It is part of the Islamic Educational Trust of Zambia and is located at Great East Road, Lusaka.
