About IETZA

The Islamic Educational Trust of Zambia (IETZA) is based at Great East Road, Lusaka. It brings together several departments, including Madrassa Rahmaniyyah, IETZA Primary School, Darul Mufazamia and Masjid-e-Luqman.

Our Foundation

Madrassa Rahmaniyyah

Madrassa Rahmaniyyah was founded in 1976 to provide Deeni Ta'leem to needy and underprivileged indigenous Zambians. Its programmes include Noorani Qa'idah, Nazrah Qur'an, Hifz-ul-Qur'an, Islamic Studies and the Aalim course.

Education and Community

What We Offer

Qur'an Education

Noorani Qa'idah, Nazrah Qur'an and Hifz-ul-Qur'an.

Islamic Studies

Including the seven-year Aalim course following the Dars-e-Nizami curriculum, started in 2022.

Formal Schooling

Zambian school education from Grade 3 to Grade 12 at Madrassa Rahmaniyyah.

Tarbiyah and Community

Character development, Anjuman-e-Rahmaniyyah and supervised Tablighi activities and community service.
